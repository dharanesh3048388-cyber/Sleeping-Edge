What is white noise?

In an ideal white-noise signal, sound power is distributed evenly per unit of frequency. To the ear it is often described as a consistent hiss. The SleepingEdge white-noise preset is generated in your browser with Web Audio; it is not a downloaded recording.

Why do people play white noise at bedtime?

A continuous sound can partially mask some steady or intermittent background noises, such as distant traffic or household sounds. Whether that makes a room feel more comfortable is personal. White noise does not remove the source of noise, guarantee uninterrupted sleep, or treat insomnia.

Does white noise improve sleep?

The research is mixed. A systematic review of continuous white or similar broadband noise found heterogeneous studies and very-low-quality evidence; results ranged from better sleep to disruption. A later review of auditory stimulation in adults did not find strong evidence that white or pink noise reliably improves sleep outcomes. It is more accurate to describe white noise as an optional masking sound than as a proven sleep treatment.

Is white noise right for everyone?

No. Some people find it distracting or unpleasant, and continuous sound can disturb sleep for others. Keep it low, do not use it to cover alarms or important sounds, and stop if it causes discomfort. You do not need a sound machine to sleep well.
