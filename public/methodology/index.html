Methodology & scientific limits

Our calculators are planning aids. They use the values a visitor enters and explicit model assumptions; they do not monitor brain activity, measure sleep stages or predict an individual's exact sleep architecture.

Sleep timing

The sleep-cycle setting is an adjustable planning assumption, not a biological constant. Sleep cycles vary between people and across the night. A suggested wake time should never be treated as more important than allowing enough total time for sleep.

Sleep duration

Sleep needs vary by age and individual circumstances. For general adult guidance, the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention recommends at least seven hours for adults aged 18–60. This is population-level guidance, not a personal prescription.

Caffeine estimates

Caffeine clearance is modelled using an assumed half-life selected by the tool. Actual metabolism varies with individual biology, medication, pregnancy, liver function, smoking and other factors. A calculated residual is an estimate, not a guarantee of sleep quality or safety.
