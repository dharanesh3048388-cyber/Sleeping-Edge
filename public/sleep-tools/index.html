Choosing the right tool

Start with the task you want to solve. A bedtime calculator estimates possible times from a schedule you choose; a nap tool compares two nap-duration options; the caffeine tool estimates residual caffeine from a dose and timing; and the seven-day tracker compares reported sleep against a personal target. None of these tools measures brain activity, diagnoses a sleep disorder or guarantees how you will feel.

How SleepingEdge approaches sleep estimates

The tools expose their assumptions instead of presenting a single time as biologically exact. Sleep-cycle intervals, time to fall asleep, caffeine half-life and personal sleep targets vary. Change the settings to explore different assumptions, and use the results as planning information rather than medical instructions.

For general sleep-duration guidance, see the CDC overview of sleep. For a description of normal sleep stages, see the National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute guide.
