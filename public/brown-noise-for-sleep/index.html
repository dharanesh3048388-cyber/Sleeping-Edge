What is brown noise?

Brown noise (also called Brownian or red noise) has a power spectrum that falls more steeply as frequency rises than pink noise. The result is a bass-heavy sound with less high-frequency hiss. The name refers to Brownian motion, not the colour of the sound. SleepingEdge synthesizes this preset in the browser.

Brown noise versus white and pink noise

White noise has roughly equal power per unit of frequency and can sound brighter. Pink noise has less high-frequency energy and often sounds smoother. Brown noise is weighted further toward low frequencies and may sound deeper. These are differences in sound character, not evidence that one colour is healthier or more effective.

Can brown noise help you sleep?

Some people prefer its lower, less hiss-like texture or use continuous sound to mask parts of the room's background noise. However, high-quality evidence that brown noise itself improves sleep is limited. Reviews of white and pink noise already report mixed or inconclusive results; those findings should not be stretched into a claim that brown noise reliably works.

Does brown noise treat ADHD or anxiety?

No such promise is made here. Anecdotes about focus or relaxation are not proof that brown noise treats ADHD, anxiety, insomnia or any health condition. The generator is an audio preference tool, not a therapy.
