Informational use only

SleepingEdge provides general educational information and estimates for planning purposes. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, emergency guidance or a substitute for consultation with a qualified health professional.

Estimates and limitations

Outputs depend on user inputs and simplified assumptions. Sleep cycles, sleep latency, caffeine metabolism and individual sleep needs vary. We do not guarantee that following a suggested time will improve sleep, prevent grogginess or produce a particular sleep stage.

Your responsibility

Use your judgement and seek qualified advice for persistent sleep problems, medication questions, pregnancy-related concerns or other health issues. Do not use the tools to make safety-critical decisions, including decisions about driving or operating machinery when tired.

Availability and changes

The tools are provided on an as-available basis and may be changed, corrected or withdrawn. We aim to keep calculations understandable but do not warrant that every estimate is complete or suitable for every person or situation.

Links to external resources are provided for convenience. Their content, availability and privacy practices are controlled by their respective operators.
