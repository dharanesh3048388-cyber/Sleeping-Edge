What does the Mountain Wind preset sound like?

The Mountain Wind preset is a synthetic soundscape built from filtered noise with slow changes in tonal character. It is intended to suggest moving air and distant gusts; it is not a recording of a mountain, forest or outdoor location.

Can wind sounds help you sleep?

People respond differently to nature sounds. A familiar or steady background can be pleasant for some, while changes in volume or texture may disturb others. There is not enough evidence to promise that this particular wind effect improves sleep or treats a sleep problem.

How to use this wind sound

Open the player with Mountain Wind selected and press Play. Start at a low volume, then adjust only to a comfortable level. Use the timer if you do not want playback to continue. Sound is optional—do not force yourself to use it if you prefer a quiet room.
