Advertising & affiliate disclosure

Last updated: 9 November 2026

SleepingEdge intends to explore advertising and affiliate partnerships. This page explains the intended disclosure standard; it does not mean that any advertising network or affiliate programme is currently active.

Affiliate links

If a link earns SleepingEdge a commission when a visitor makes a qualifying purchase, it will be clearly identified near the link. A commission should not increase the price paid by the visitor unless the merchant's own terms say otherwise. We will aim to recommend products only when relevant to the page and its readers.

Advertising

Paid advertising will be distinguishable from editorial content. Advertisers will not be allowed to buy favourable calculator results, scientific conclusions or undisclosed recommendations.

Editorial independence

Commercial relationships should not determine the assumptions or conclusions in our sleep-science explanations. Any material relationship relevant to a recommendation will be disclosed on that page.

Current status

No publisher account ID, affiliate tracking ID or advertising script is configured in this release. This disclosure and the privacy notice must be reviewed when a real programme is selected and before tracking or ads are enabled.

SleepingEdge provides informational sleep-planning tools, not medical diagnosis or treatment. Return to the calculator.
