Advertising & affiliate disclosure

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Current commercial status

There are currently no active display advertisements, ad network scripts, paid sponsorships, affiliate links or affiliate tracking IDs on SleepingEdge. This page explains the standards that will apply if commercial relationships are introduced; it does not mean a programme is active or that a link on this site currently earns a commission.

Advertising

If display advertising is introduced, ads will be clearly labelled (for example, “Advertisement” or “Sponsored”) and visually distinguishable from SleepingEdge content, navigation, calculator controls and results. We will not disguise ads as buttons, recommendations, downloads or editorial content, encourage visitors to click ads, or place ads where they could cause accidental interaction with a tool. Ad placement will be reviewed against the advertising provider's current policies, including restrictions on pages with insufficient content.

Ad networks may select or serve advertisements using their own systems and policies. Any future advertising provider, relevant technologies and data practices will be described in the Privacy Policy before the ads are activated. SleepingEdge will not intentionally pass calculator inputs, sleep schedules, caffeine quantities, individual results or inferred health details to advertising partners, and will not use sensitive health-related information to personalise advertising.

Affiliate links and commissions

If a link is an affiliate link and a visitor makes a qualifying purchase through it, SleepingEdge may earn a commission. Where that applies, the relationship will be disclosed clearly and conspicuously close to the relevant recommendation or link, using plain language such as “We may earn a commission if you buy through this link.” A short label such as “paid link” or “ad” may also be used where appropriate. A disclosure will not be hidden only in this page or in general site terms.

A commission generally does not change the price set by the merchant, but product prices, availability, delivery, returns, warranties and purchase terms are controlled by the merchant and may change. Visitors should review the merchant's terms before buying. SleepingEdge does not process payments or orders for third-party merchants.

Retailer-specific programme rules

If SleepingEdge joins Amazon Associates, it will display the exact programme statement required by Amazon — “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.” — clearly and conspicuously on the site, and will also place clear disclosures near applicable affiliate links. This statement is not a claim that SleepingEdge is currently an Amazon Associate. Other affiliate programmes may have their own wording, link-format, price-display and placement rules, which will be followed before their links are published.

Editorial independence and reviews

Payment, commissions or sponsorship will not buy a favourable product review, scientific conclusion, calculator result, sleep recommendation or ranking. Where a commercial relationship is relevant to a review or recommendation, it will be disclosed on that page. We will aim to explain the basis of recommendations and distinguish personal opinion, product claims and independently sourced evidence. We will not claim a product is clinically proven or medically endorsed without appropriate evidence.

Corrections and concerns

If an advertisement, sponsorship or affiliate disclosure appears unclear or incorrect, please contact us through the Contact page.

This page describes the intended disclosure standard, not legal advice or a certification of compliance. Applicable advertising, consumer-protection, platform and affiliate-programme requirements will be reviewed for the actual operator and markets before monetisation is enabled.
