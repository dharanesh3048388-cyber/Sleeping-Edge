Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Important scope note

This policy describes the current GitHub Pages release of SleepingEdge. It is not a statement that advertising, analytics, affiliate tracking, accounts or a consent-management platform are already active. This policy must be revised before any such service is enabled. The site operator's legal identity, jurisdiction and private privacy-request contact have not yet been supplied; those details must be added and this policy reviewed before commercial launch.

1. Who operates SleepingEdge

SleepingEdge is a sleep-planning and educational website. The legal name, business address, operating jurisdiction and private contact details of the site operator have not been provided for publication. We will not invent these details. They must be published here before the site begins collecting information through advertising, analytics, affiliate tracking, accounts or other commercial services.

2. Information you enter into calculators

The core calculators are designed to perform calculations in your browser. The current site does not require an account and does not intentionally transmit sleep or wake times, nap choices, caffeine quantities or calculator results to a SleepingEdge application server. Do not treat this as a medical-record service or enter information you would not want processed by your own browser or the hosting infrastructure.

3. Information processed when you visit

The website is delivered through GitHub Pages, a third-party static hosting service. The hosting provider and its infrastructure providers may process technical information associated with requests, such as IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, timestamps, referrer information and security or diagnostic logs. Their processing is governed by the provider's applicable terms and privacy information. SleepingEdge does not control the host's infrastructure logs or retention practices.

4. Browser storage and preferences

The website may use browser storage, including local storage, to remember interface or calculator preferences on your device. This information remains in your browser unless a browser feature, extension, backup or other software handles it differently. You can clear site data through your browser settings. Clearing it or using private browsing may remove or prevent saved preferences. The current site does not use a SleepingEdge account system to sync those preferences across devices.

5. Fonts and other third-party resources

The current homepage loads fonts from Google Fonts. A request for an externally hosted font may disclose technical request information, including your IP address, to the provider. See the Google Privacy Policy. The site is delivered through GitHub Pages; the hosting provider's privacy terms apply to technical request data. External websites linked from SleepingEdge have their own privacy practices.

6. Advertising, affiliate links and analytics

As of the date of this policy, this production release does not intentionally load an AdSense advertising script, affiliate tracking code or a SleepingEdge analytics service. No publisher ID, affiliate ID or analytics measurement ID is configured.

If these services are introduced, this policy will be updated before activation to identify the actual providers, information collected, purposes, cookies or similar technologies, retention and sharing practices, international transfers where relevant, and available choices. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalise advertisements, subject to the configuration and applicable law. Affiliate programmes may record that a visitor followed a qualifying link or completed a purchase. Analytics providers may collect usage and device information. We will not intentionally send raw sleep times, caffeine amounts or individual calculator results to advertising or analytics providers.

7. Cookies, consent and your choices

The current site does not intentionally deploy an advertising or analytics cookie system. Browser storage used for preferences is described above. If non-essential cookies, advertising technologies or analytics are added, the site must implement an appropriate consent and preference mechanism for the regions it serves before those technologies are activated. Your browser may also provide controls to block or delete cookies and local storage; doing so can affect site functionality.

8. How information may be shared

At present, technical requests are handled by the hosting and resource providers described above. If advertising, analytics, affiliate, security, support or other services are added, the policy will identify the actual providers and the purposes for which information is disclosed. We do not claim that no third party receives technical information when you visit this website.

9. International visitors

SleepingEdge is intended for a global audience. Privacy rights and obligations differ by country and may depend on where you live and how the service is operated. Where laws such as the EU or UK GDPR, applicable US state privacy laws, India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework or other local rules apply, the operator must provide the notices, lawful basis, choices, rights process and safeguards required for that situation. This general policy does not determine which law applies to a particular visitor or promise that every statutory right is available in every location.

10. Children

SleepingEdge is a general informational tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The site operator must establish and publish any age-related rules and required parental-consent process before introducing accounts, advertising or tracking. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through a channel controlled by the site, use a private contact method once published; do not post the information in a public issue.

11. Security and retention

We aim to keep the site and its dependencies maintained, but no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. The current calculator design does not create a SleepingEdge server-side account or calculator-history database. Hosting providers may retain technical logs under their own policies. Retention periods for any future analytics, advertising, affiliate or support services will be disclosed when those services are selected and configured.

12. Your privacy choices and requests

A private email address or other private privacy-request channel has not yet been supplied. The Contact page does not currently provide a private privacy-request channel. Do not submit personal, health or identity information through public comments or unrelated third-party channels. A private request channel and the operator identity must be added before commercial launch and before relying on this policy as the complete privacy notice.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy will need updating when the domain, operator, hosting, storage, analytics, advertising, affiliate services or legal requirements change. The revision date above will be changed when a substantive update is published.
