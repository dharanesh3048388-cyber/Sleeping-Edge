Privacy notice

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This notice describes the current GitHub Pages version of SleepingEdge. It must be reviewed and updated before adding analytics, advertising, affiliate tracking, accounts or other third-party services.

Calculator inputs

The core calculators are designed to process entered values in your browser. The current site does not require an account and does not intentionally send sleep times, caffeine amounts or calculator results to a SleepingEdge application server.

Local browser storage

The site may use browser local storage to remember display and calculator preferences on your device. You can clear this data through your browser settings. Private browsing, browser restrictions or clearing site data may prevent preferences from being retained.

Hosting and third-party resources

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub and infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP address, request details and security logs under their own policies. The current homepage also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which can result in requests to Google. Review the GitHub Pages information and Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics and advertising

As of the date above, this production release does not intentionally load a SleepingEdge analytics service or ad network. If analytics, advertising, affiliate tracking or consent-management services are introduced, this notice must be updated before activation, including details about providers, purposes, choices and applicable regional requirements.

Children and sensitive information

Do not submit medical records, personal health details or other sensitive information through public issue trackers or feedback channels. The site is not designed to collect such information.

Questions

For site feedback or to report a privacy concern, use the contact page. Do not include sensitive personal or health information in a public report.

SleepingEdge provides informational sleep-planning tools, not medical diagnosis or treatment. Return to the calculator.
