Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Important scope note

This policy describes the current GitHub Pages release of SleepingEdge. No advertising or affiliate tracking is enabled. Optional Google Analytics 4 is supported but remains inactive until the owner configures a real measurement ID; the GA4 tag is not loaded and no analytics data is sent unless a visitor explicitly accepts. The site operator's legal identity, business address and operating jurisdiction have not yet been supplied. A public email contact is provided on the Contact page; this does not itself identify the legal operator.

1. Who operates SleepingEdge

SleepingEdge is a sleep-planning and educational website. The legal name, business address and operating jurisdiction of the site operator have not been provided for publication. We will not invent these details. A public email contact is provided on the Contact page. The operator identity and jurisdiction still need to be added and this policy reviewed before commercial launch.

2. Information you enter into calculators

The core calculators are designed to perform calculations in your browser. The current site does not require an account and does not intentionally transmit sleep or wake times, nap choices, caffeine quantities or calculator results to a SleepingEdge application server. Do not treat this as a medical-record service or enter information you would not want processed by your own browser or the hosting infrastructure.

3. Information processed when you visit

The website is delivered through GitHub Pages, a third-party static hosting service. The hosting provider and its infrastructure providers may process technical information associated with requests, such as IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, timestamps, referrer information and security or diagnostic logs. Their processing is governed by the provider's applicable terms and privacy information. SleepingEdge does not control the host's infrastructure logs or retention practices.

4. Browser storage and preferences

The website may use browser storage, including local storage, to remember interface or calculator preferences on your device. This information remains in your browser unless a browser feature, extension, backup or other software handles it differently. You can clear site data through your browser settings. Clearing it or using private browsing may remove or prevent saved preferences. The current site does not use a SleepingEdge account system to sync those preferences across devices.

5. Fonts and other third-party resources

The current homepage loads fonts from Google Fonts. A request for an externally hosted font may disclose technical request information, including your IP address, to the provider. See the Google Privacy Policy. The site is delivered through GitHub Pages; the hosting provider's privacy terms apply to technical request data. External websites linked from SleepingEdge have their own privacy practices.

6. Advertising, affiliate links and analytics

As of the date of this policy, no AdSense advertising script, affiliate tracking code or Google Analytics 4 measurement ID is configured. Optional GA4 can be activated only when the owner adds a valid measurement ID to deployment settings. If activated, the Google tag is not downloaded and no analytics data is sent until a visitor explicitly accepts optional analytics. Visitors who reject are not tracked by this site's GA4 integration.

If optional Google Analytics 4 is configured and a visitor accepts, Google may process standard page-view and associated technical usage information, such as page URL, browser/device information and approximate location derived from IP address, under Google's own terms and privacy practices. The site configures advertising storage, advertising user-data and advertising personalisation as denied, and sends only standard page views. SleepingEdge does not intentionally send raw sleep times, caffeine amounts, calculator inputs or individual results. Google Analytics data retention and account-level settings must be reviewed by the operator. No GA4 data is sent by this integration when a visitor rejects or has not yet chosen.

7. Contact emails

If you choose to use the Contact page, your email application opens with a pre-filled recipient and subject. You decide whether to send the message. When you send it, your email address, message content, any attachments you choose to include, and the technical information handled by your email provider are transmitted through that provider to the recipient mailbox at IndianCreativeStudios@outlook.com. The email is handled and stored in the recipient's Outlook mailbox and may be retained for support, correspondence and privacy-request handling. The website does not receive the email through a form, does not operate a contact-message database and does not automatically receive a copy. Your use of Gmail, Outlook or another email provider is also subject to that provider's privacy terms. Avoid sending sensitive personal or medical information.

8. Cookies, consent and your choices

Google Analytics is optional and loads only after explicit acceptance. The site stores the visitor's choice ('accepted' or 'rejected') in local storage under a first-party preference key so the choice can be remembered. Visitors can change that choice using the Analytics preferences link in the footer. If a visitor rejects or has not chosen, the GA4 tag is not loaded by this integration. This is a basic opt-in implementation, not a legal compliance certification; the operator must review applicable regional rules before enabling analytics. Your browser can block or clear local storage and cookies; clearing site data may cause the choice prompt to appear again.

9. How information may be shared

At present, technical requests are handled by the hosting and resource providers described above. If advertising, analytics, affiliate, security, support or other services are added, the policy will identify the actual providers and the purposes for which information is disclosed. We do not claim that no third party receives technical information when you visit this website.

10. International visitors

SleepingEdge is intended for a global audience. Privacy rights and obligations differ by country and may depend on where you live and how the service is operated. Where laws such as the EU or UK GDPR, applicable US state privacy laws, India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework or other local rules apply, the operator must provide the notices, lawful basis, choices, rights process and safeguards required for that situation. This general policy does not determine which law applies to a particular visitor or promise that every statutory right is available in every location.

11. Children

SleepingEdge is a general informational tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The site operator must establish and publish any age-related rules and required parental-consent process before introducing accounts, advertising or tracking. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through a channel controlled by the site, use a private contact method once published; do not post the information in a public issue.

12. Security and retention

We aim to keep the site and its dependencies maintained, but no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. The current calculator design does not create a SleepingEdge server-side account or calculator-history database. Hosting providers may retain technical logs under their own policies. Retention periods for any future analytics, advertising, affiliate or support services will be disclosed when those services are selected and configured.

13. Your privacy choices and requests

For a privacy question or request, use the email address on the Contact page and put “Privacy request” in the subject. We will need enough information to understand and respond to the request; please do not send identity documents or sensitive personal information unless specifically requested through an appropriate secure process. The operator identity, jurisdiction and any statutory rights-handling process still require completion and legal review before commercial launch.

14. Changes to this policy

This policy will need updating when the domain, operator, hosting, storage, analytics, advertising, affiliate services or legal requirements change. The revision date above will be changed when a substantive update is published.
