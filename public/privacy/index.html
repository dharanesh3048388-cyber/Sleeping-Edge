Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Current release at a glance
  • Sleep and calculator values are processed in your browser; the site does not intentionally send those values or results to a SleepingEdge application server.
  • No account, server-side calculator-history database, advertising script, affiliate tracking code or ad network is currently configured.
  • Optional Google Analytics 4 is inactive until the owner configures a measurement ID. When configured, the tag loads only after an affirmative analytics choice.
  • The legal name, business address and operating jurisdiction of the project operator have not yet been supplied. This policy is a working notice, not a legal compliance certification.

1. Who operates the website

SleepingEdge is an independent digital project. The operator's legal name, business address and operating jurisdiction have not yet been supplied for publication; we will not invent these details. For questions or privacy requests, use the email contact on the Contact page. Operator details and the applicable legal framework must be confirmed and this notice reviewed before commercial launch.

2. Calculator inputs and results

The core calculators are designed to perform calculations in your browser. The current site does not intentionally transmit sleep or wake times, nap choices, caffeine quantities, personal settings or calculator results to a SleepingEdge application server. The site does not require an account and does not maintain a SleepingEdge server-side calculator-history database. Your browser, extensions, device software and hosting infrastructure remain outside our control. Do not enter information you would not want processed by your own device.

3. Hosting and technical information

The website is delivered through GitHub Pages. When your browser requests pages and assets, the hosting provider and its infrastructure providers may process technical information associated with those requests, such as IP address, browser and device details, requested URLs, timestamps, referrer information and security or diagnostic logs. Their processing, security measures and retention are governed by their applicable terms and privacy notices. SleepingEdge does not control the provider's infrastructure logs or set their retention period.

4. Browser storage and preferences

The site may use browser local storage to remember interface preferences, including the selected theme and, if optional analytics is configured, your analytics choice. These values are stored by your browser on your device and are not a SleepingEdge account. You can clear them through your browser's site-data settings; doing so may reset preferences or cause a consent prompt to appear again. Private browsing or storage restrictions may prevent preferences from being saved.

5. Fonts and other third-party resources

The current homepage requests fonts from Google Fonts. A browser request to an external resource can disclose technical request information, including your IP address and browser details, to that provider. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. External sites linked from SleepingEdge have their own privacy practices. Review the relevant provider notices before using those services.

6. Analytics

No Google Analytics 4 measurement ID is currently configured in the production deployment, so the optional GA4 integration is inactive. If the owner configures GA4, the tag is not downloaded and analytics data is not sent by this integration until a visitor affirmatively accepts optional analytics. If accepted, Google may process standard page-view and associated technical usage information, such as page URL and browser/device details, under Google's terms and privacy practices. The integration is configured to deny advertising storage, advertising user-data and advertising personalisation; the owner must also keep GA4 Enhanced Measurement switched off to maintain the intended page-view-only setup. SleepingEdge does not intentionally read or transmit raw sleep schedules, caffeine amounts, calculator inputs or individual results to GA4.

When the integration is active, visitors can reject optional analytics or later change their choice using the Analytics preferences control in the footer. That control is injected by the analytics script only when a real GA4 measurement ID is configured. If GA4 is not configured, no analytics prompt or analytics-preferences control is needed because no GA4 tag is loaded.

7. Advertising and affiliate links

No advertising network, ad-serving script, publisher ID, affiliate tracking code or paid sponsorship is active in this release. If advertising or affiliate services are introduced, this policy will be updated before activation to identify the actual providers, categories of information processed, cookies or similar technologies, purposes, recipients, retention settings, relevant user choices and any required consent mechanism. Third-party advertising providers may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or device identifiers to deliver or measure ads, as described in the updated notice and their own policies.

SleepingEdge will not intentionally send calculator inputs or results, sleep schedules, caffeine amounts or inferred health details to advertising partners, and will not use sensitive health-related information to personalise advertising. Any ad or affiliate implementation must be reviewed against the selected provider's current publisher rules and the privacy laws applicable to the visitors served. See the Advertising & affiliate disclosure.

8. Cookies, consent and choices

The current release does not include an advertising cookie or affiliate-tracking cookie from SleepingEdge. The optional analytics integration stores the visitor's choice in first-party local storage and loads GA4 only after affirmative acceptance. If advertising technologies are added, cookies and similar technologies used by the ad provider, their purposes and available controls will be described here before activation. A basic analytics choice is not an advertising consent platform and is not a substitute for any region-specific consent mechanism required by an ad network or applicable law.

9. Email correspondence

If you choose to use the Contact page, your email application opens with a pre-filled recipient and subject. You decide whether to send the message. When you send it, your email address, message content, attachments you choose to include and technical information handled by your email provider are transmitted through that provider to the recipient mailbox at IndianCreativeStudios@outlook.com. The message is handled and stored in the recipient's email account and may be retained for correspondence, support and privacy-request handling. The website does not receive the message through a form, does not operate a contact-message database and does not automatically receive a copy. Your email provider's privacy terms also apply. Avoid sending medical records, passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

10. How information is shared

Technical requests are handled by the hosting and external-resource providers described above. If new analytics, advertising, affiliate, security or support providers are added, their actual role and data access will be disclosed in this policy before activation where required. We do not claim that no third party receives technical information when you visit this website.

The scope, exceptions and response process vary by law. To make a privacy request, use the Contact page and put “Privacy request” in the subject. We will assess and respond under the law that applies; this general statement does not promise that every right is available in every location or that a particular legal regime applies to every visitor.

The operator must review applicable requirements before commercial launch, including relevant Indian data-protection requirements and their phased commencement, and any applicable EU/UK, US state or other regional privacy rules. This page is not a legal determination of jurisdiction or compliance.

12. Children and sensitive information

SleepingEdge is a general informational tool and is not designed as a service for collecting children's personal information or managing health records. Do not submit medical records or sensitive personal information through the contact channel. If future features are directed to children or collect their personal data, the operator must review applicable age, notice, consent and advertising restrictions before launch.

We aim to maintain the website and its dependencies, but no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Email correspondence may remain in the recipient's mailbox. If analytics or other services are enabled, their retention settings and periods will be disclosed or linked here once selected and configured.
