About SleepingEdge

SleepingEdge is an independent project offering browser-based tools for planning sleep and wake times, exploring nap timing, estimating caffeine clearance and reviewing sleep-debt arithmetic.

Our product principles
  • Show assumptions instead of presenting estimates as facts.
  • Prioritise adequate sleep opportunity over chasing an exact cycle boundary.
  • Keep core calculations in the visitor's browser and avoid requiring an account.
  • Explain limitations clearly and never present a calculator result as a diagnosis.

Independence

SleepingEdge is not a medical provider and is not affiliated with the organisations whose educational resources may be referenced. The site is an informational tool, not a substitute for advice from a qualified health professional.
