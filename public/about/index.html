About SleepingEdge

Last updated: 11 November 2026

SleepingEdge is an independent digital project offering browser-based tools for planning sleep and wake times, comparing nap opportunities, estimating caffeine clearance, reviewing sleep-debt arithmetic and listening to generated ambient sounds.

What the tools are — and are not
  • Core calculations run in the visitor's browser and do not require an account.
  • Outputs are estimates based on the inputs and assumptions shown, not measurements of a person's sleep.
  • The site is educational and informational; it is not a clinic, hospital, sleep laboratory or medical provider.
  • No calculator result should be used to diagnose a condition, change medication or decide whether someone is safe to drive or operate machinery.

Evidence and editorial standards

We aim to explain assumptions, link to primary or authoritative sources, distinguish evidence from simplified calculator models, and correct material errors when identified. Our Methodology & evidence page explains the sources and limitations behind the tools. SleepingEdge is not currently clinician-reviewed or endorsed by a named sleep specialist. We will only claim professional review after a genuine review and verification of the reviewer's qualifications and permission to be named.

Privacy by design

The calculators are designed to process the values you enter locally in your browser. The site does not require an account or maintain a SleepingEdge server-side calculator-history database. Hosting and any optional third-party services may still process technical visit information; see the Privacy Policy for the current data-flow description.

Independence and commercial support

SleepingEdge is intended to remain useful without a purchase. Commercial relationships, including advertising or affiliate partnerships if introduced, will be disclosed clearly and kept separate from calculator logic, evidence summaries and editorial conclusions. No advertiser or merchant may pay to change a calculation, scientific explanation or result. The current release has no active advertising network, paid sponsorship or affiliate tracking. See Advertising & affiliate disclosure.

Who operates the project

SleepingEdge is currently published as an independent project. The legal name of the operator, registered business details and operating jurisdiction have not yet been supplied for publication. We will not invent or imply a company, medical organisation or professional credential. The public contact channel is listed on the Contact page. The operator details and jurisdiction must be completed and reviewed before commercial advertising, affiliate transactions or other commercial services are activated.

Corrections and contact

If you notice a calculation issue, broken link, accessibility problem or factual error, please contact us using the Contact page. Please do not send medical records or other sensitive information.
