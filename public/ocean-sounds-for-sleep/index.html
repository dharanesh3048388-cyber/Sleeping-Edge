What is the Deep Ocean sound?

This preset combines filtered noise, a slow modulation and a low tone to create a rolling, underwater-inspired sound. It is an artistic soundscape, not a recording of the sea and not a calibrated underwater or infrasonic simulation.

Why choose ocean sounds?

Some listeners enjoy slow, repeating waves or low, even background textures as part of a bedtime routine. Preferences differ: a low rumble can feel soothing to one person but intrusive to another. Ocean audio may also mask some environmental changes, but it cannot remove noise or guarantee uninterrupted sleep.

What does the research say?

Studies of ambient sound as a sleep aid are varied and do not establish a reliable benefit for every listener. A 2025 review describes evidence for ambient noises such as white and pink noise as inconclusive; results for one generated ocean-style sound should not be inferred from studies of other sounds.
