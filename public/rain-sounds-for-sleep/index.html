What does the SleepingEdge rain sound contain?

The Midnight Rain on Glass preset is procedurally generated from filtered noise with small, irregular changes in texture. It is designed as a continuous rain-inspired soundscape. It is not a recording of a particular storm, room, roof or window.

Why listen to rain at bedtime?

Some people enjoy a consistent natural-sounding background while they wind down. A steady sound may also make certain changes in the surrounding noise less noticeable. The effect is individual: rain sounds can be calming for one person and distracting for another.

Is rain sound proven to improve sleep?

Research on digital sound aids is still developing. Recent reviews describe evidence for ambient sounds as inconclusive, and existing noise studies do not establish that a particular rain track reliably improves sleep for everyone. SleepingEdge does not claim that rain sounds change sleep stages, treat insomnia or guarantee faster sleep onset.
