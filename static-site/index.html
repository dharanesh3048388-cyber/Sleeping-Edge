How sleep timing estimates work

Sleep is organized into repeating NREM and REM patterns, but cycle length is not fixed at 90 minutes. It varies between people and across the same night. A clock-based calculator cannot know which stage you will be in when an alarm sounds.

This tool offers several durations based on illustrative 90-minute intervals. Treat them as planning options, not medical recommendations or a promise that waking at a particular time will prevent sleep inertia.

For most adults, regularly allowing at least seven hours of sleep is a useful starting point. Individual needs vary with age, health, workload and other factors. If you are persistently sleepy or struggling to sleep, seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
