What this weekly sleep comparison can tell you

The calculator subtracts your recorded sleep from the weekly total implied by the target you enter. It is a simple comparison, not a clinical measurement of accumulated sleep debt or a prediction of how quickly you can recover.

Sleep needs differ between people, and a single week of estimates cannot capture sleep quality, timing, illness, shift work or longer-term patterns. Avoid treating the calculated number as a precise amount that must be repaid.

A consistent schedule and adequate opportunity for sleep are sensible starting points. If you have persistent daytime sleepiness or difficulty sleeping, seek professional advice.
