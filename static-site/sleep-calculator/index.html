Sleep & Bedtime Calculator

Explore bedtime or wake-time options using a sleep-onset estimate and adjustable planning intervals. Prioritize enough total sleep; the calculator cannot predict your personal sleep stages.

Planning estimates, not medical advice: The interval used by this tool is an adjustable arithmetic assumption. Sleep cycles differ between people and across the night. These results do not identify sleep stages, predict sleep inertia, or guarantee that a particular wake time will feel better.

How to use sleep timing estimates

Choose whether you have a target wake time, are planning to try sleeping now, or have a planned bedtime. The calculator adds the sleep-onset buffer you enter and displays several illustrative intervals.

Human sleep cycles are not fixed at exactly 90 minutes. Their duration and the balance of non-REM and REM sleep change through the night and vary from person to person. A clock-based calculator cannot determine whether you are in light sleep or deep sleep when an alarm sounds.

Prioritize sufficient sleep opportunity

For most adults, regularly allowing at least seven hours of sleep is a useful general starting point, although individual sleep needs vary. Do not shorten sleep solely to match a calculator's interval. Consistent schedules and adequate sleep opportunity matter more than trying to time an assumed cycle boundary.

About the fall-asleep estimate

The sleep-onset buffer is a personal planning input, not a clinical measurement. Adjust it to reflect your own estimate, and remember that it can vary from night to night.
