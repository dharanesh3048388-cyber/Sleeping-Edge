Privacy Policy

Preview policy · Last reviewed November 2026

This policy describes the current SleepingEdge preview website. It should be updated if analytics, advertising, contact forms, accounts or other third-party services are added.

Calculator inputs

The calculators run in your browser. The values you enter are processed by JavaScript on your device and are not intentionally sent to a SleepingEdge application server or stored in a SleepingEdge database.

Browser storage

The site may save your display-theme preference in your browser’s local storage so that your choice is remembered on later visits. You can clear this data through your browser settings. The calculators do not need an account.

Hosting and technical logs

This preview is delivered through a third-party static hosting provider. When your browser requests website files, the provider and its infrastructure partners may process technical information such as IP address, request details and browser information under their applicable privacy terms.

Analytics, advertising and contact forms

The current preview does not intentionally load an analytics service, serve advertising, use affiliate tracking links or provide a working message-submission form. If these features are introduced, this policy and any required consent controls must be updated before launch.

Children and sensitive information

Do not enter personal, medical or otherwise sensitive information into the preview. It is not designed to collect or manage health records.

Policy changes

This policy may change as the site develops. The published date should be updated when material changes are made.
