Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 8, 2026 · Effective Immediately

Welcome to SleepingEdge ("we," "our," or "us"), accessible from https://YOURDOMAIN.com. We are committed to transparency and respecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how it is processed, and your legal rights under regulations including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1. Information We Do Not Collect

All sleep cycle calculations, caffeine decay simulations, nap alarms, and sleep debt inputs performed on SleepingEdge execute entirely on your local browser (client-side). We do not store, log, transmit, or sell your personal sleep habits, schedules, or calculator inputs to external servers or databases.

2. Analytics & Telemetry (Plausible Analytics)

We use Plausible Analytics to measure overall website traffic, page popularity, and calculator usage events. Plausible is a lightweight, privacy-first open-source analytics platform. Key privacy protections include:

  • No Cookies: Plausible does not use cookies or any persistent device identifiers.
  • No Personal Data: IP addresses are never logged or stored. Aggregate page requests are processed in compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and PECR.
  • No Cross-Site Tracking: Your browsing activity across other websites is never monitored.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

A cookie is a small text file saved to your device. We use browser localStorage and minimal cookies for the following specific purposes:

  • Strictly Necessary: Storing your display theme preference (dark or light mode) via sleepingedge_theme and your cookie consent status via sleepingedge_cookie_consent.
  • Advertising Partners: If you grant advertising consent, third-party advertising vendors (such as Google AdSense or advertising exchanges) may deploy cookies to serve relevant contextual advertisements and prevent fraud.

You can modify or withdraw your cookie consent at any time by clicking the Cookie Preferences link in our website footer.

4. Advertising Partners & Google AdSense

To maintain SleepingEdge as a free public resource, we display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners. These partners may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on prior visits to our website or other internet sites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to SleepingEdge and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Switzerland will be presented with an interactive consent dialogue to accept or reject non-essential personalized advertising cookies.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or by visiting aboutads.info.

SleepingEdge is a participant in affiliate marketing programs, including the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for websites to earn advertising fees by linking to Amazon.com and affiliated sites.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your geographic jurisdiction, you hold specific statutory rights regarding your data:

  • Right of Access & Rectification: You may request confirmation of whether your personal data is processed and obtain a copy.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You may request the deletion of any personal data we hold.
  • Right to Opt-Out of the Sale of Personal Data (CCPA): We do not sell personal information as defined by California law.
