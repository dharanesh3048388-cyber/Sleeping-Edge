Sleep stages and sleep efficiency are different measures

Sleep efficiency is a simple ratio: estimated time asleep divided by time in bed. It does not reveal how much time you spent in deep sleep or REM, and it cannot determine whether your sleep was restorative.

Actual sleep stages are measured using clinical sleep studies. A calculator based on self-reported time in bed, sleep onset and awakenings cannot reconstruct those stages.

Use the result as a rough personal estimate only. If you have persistent sleep problems, significant daytime sleepiness or concerns about a sleep disorder, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
