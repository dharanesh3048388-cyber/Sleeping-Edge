About SleepingEdge

Dedicated to Human Recovery, Chronobiology & Sleep Science

SleepingEdge was founded with a singular conviction: human physical recovery, cognitive clarity, and emotional resilience start with synchronized sleep architecture.

In an era dominated by artificial LED blue light, late-night digital notifications, and round-the-clock caffeine consumption, modern schedules are severely disconnected from human evolutionary biology. Millions of people wake up each morning suffering from chronic sleep inertia simply because their alarm sounded during the middle of a slow-wave deep sleep cycle.

Our Mission

We provide 100% free, evidence-based circadian calculators that empower individuals to take control of their nocturnal biology. By transforming complex peer-reviewed sleep literature into intuitive, lightning-fast digital tools, SleepingEdge helps students, shift workers, athletes, and professionals optimize:

  • Bedtime & Wake Timing: Aligning alarms with the natural termination of 90-minute ultradian cycles.
  • Caffeine Clearance: Calculating personalized caffeine curfews based on liver elimination pharmacokinetics.
  • Strategic Afternoon Napping: Utilizing NASA pilot protocols to refresh working memory without post-nap grogginess.
  • Sleep Debt Mitigation: Repaying accumulated fatigue safely without circadian phase disruption.

Scientific Framework & Guidelines

Our algorithms and educational content are strictly informed by consensus benchmarks established by world-renowned sleep research organizations:

  • American Academy of Sleep Medicine (AASM): Clinical sleep latency benchmarks and polysomnographic stage definitions.
  • National Sleep Foundation (NSF): Age-stratified sleep duration guidelines and sleep efficiency standards.
  • NASA Ames Fatigue Countermeasures Program: Dr. Mark Rosekind's operational flight protocols on 26-minute nap efficacy.
  • Pharmacokinetic Clearance Models: First-order exponential decay models of caffeine antagonism at adenosine A1 and A2A receptors.

Editorial Independence & Transparency

SleepingEdge is an independent publisher. We do not accept sponsored content or pay-to-play product reviews. When we recommend physical sleep hygiene products (such as blackout masks or noise generators), each recommendation is vetted against circadian principles. We clearly disclose all affiliate relationships in compliance with FTC guidelines.
