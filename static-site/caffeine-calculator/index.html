Understanding caffeine timing

Caffeine is cleared gradually, and its half-life varies considerably between people. A simple first-order model estimates the amount remaining over time, but it cannot account for every medication, health condition, pregnancy-related change or individual sensitivity.

The calculator’s residual-milligram reference is an illustrative modelling choice only. It is not a clinically established safe threshold, and reaching it does not guarantee that caffeine will not affect your sleep.

If caffeine affects your sleep, consider reducing the dose or moving consumption earlier. Ask a healthcare professional about caffeine use if you are pregnant, have a relevant medical condition or take medicines that may interact with it.
